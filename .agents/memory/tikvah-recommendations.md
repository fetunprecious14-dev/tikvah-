---
name: Tikvah resource recommendations
description: Recommendation behavior for emotion-matched journal resources.
---

Recommendations are local, emotion-matched, and deliberately small: at most three existing Tikvah resources are selected after safety screening and emotion classification, then stored with the encrypted entry.

**Why:** Suggestions should reduce decision fatigue and preserve privacy; they must feel like invitations rather than prescriptions or a content feed.

**How to apply:** Keep the catalog curated and destinations valid. Never recommend resources for a high-risk draft, and never let recommendations override crisis support or become advice.
